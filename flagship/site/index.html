Flagship emerged in Charlotte, North Carolina, in 2011, bringing together songwriter Drake Margolnick and drummer Michael Finster around a shared musical direction.

Drawing from alternative rock and expansive, atmospheric arrangements, the project developed its identity through early releases, studio albums, and a growing presence on the live circuit.

From the Blackbush EP to the self-titled album, Faded, and The Electric Man, Flagship continued to build a body of work that reflects its creative evolution. The project also took its music on the road, performing throughout the United States and internationally.

Drake Margolnick later explored a solo chapter under his own name before returning his focus to Flagship.

Today, Flagship brings its history and musical identity into a new phase, connecting its existing catalog with the work still to come.

      About Flagship

      Flagship is an alternative rock project led by singer-songwriter Drake Margolnick, rooted in Charlotte, North Carolina.

      Formed in 2011, Flagship developed a distinctive sound shaped by atmospheric textures, driving rock energy, and expansive melodies. Over the years, the project has released a series of records that reflect its musical evolution, including Blackbush, Flagship, Faded, The Electric Man, and The Ladder.

      With a history of touring across the United States, Europe, and Canada, Flagship has shared stages with established artists and performed at major music festivals, building a connection with audiences through its recorded work and live performances.

      After exploring a solo chapter under the name Margolnick, Drake returns to Flagship as the central focus of his musical journey — bringing the project's identity forward while continuing to develop its sound.
