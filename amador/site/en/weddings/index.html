Weddings

Live violin for your wedding

You can book live violin for one part of the day or enquire about combining several parts of the wedding. Tell me how you’ve planned the day and what songs you’d like to hear.

Check my date WhatsApp
01

Ceremony

We can choose a piece for the entrance and others for the moments in your ceremony schedule. If you have a special song in mind, send me the title and the version you love.

02

Drinks and reception

During drinks, the violin can sit comfortably behind conversation or build towards a livelier atmosphere. At the reception, we can save the music for your entrance, dinner or the moment the dancing starts.

Ideas for the day

A piece for every moment

Suggestions from my recorded repertoire. The final choice is always yours.

Have a special song?

Send me the title and version when you enquire about your date, and we’ll check whether it can be prepared for your wedding.

Suggest a song

  1. 01 · Walking down the aisle

    Palladio · Experience × Interstellar

    For the moment all eyes are on you.

  2. 02 · Signing

    Mariage d’amour · Only Time

    Calm music while you sign the register.

  3. 03 · Exit

    Until I Found You · All You Need Is Love

    More light and rhythm as newlyweds.

  4. 04 · Drinks

    Sarà perché ti amo · Felicità · Corazón partío

    Conversation, toasts and songs everyone knows.

  5. 05 · Reception entrance

    Conquest of Paradise · Now We Are Free

    An entrance with a cinematic feel.

  6. 06 · Dinner

    Caruso · Right Here Waiting

    In the background, without interrupting the table.

  7. 07 · First dance & party

    Don’t Stop Me Now · Paradise

    The violin opens the dance floor.
