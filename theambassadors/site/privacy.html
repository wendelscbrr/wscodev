Your data, your choice

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Who we are

This is the official website of The Ambassadors Live Show, a live band based in Italy, which acts as data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679). For any privacy question or request, write to theambassadors.liveshow@gmail.com.

2. What data we handle

  • Booking form: name, email, type of event, show format, event date, city, country, estimated guests and the message you write. The website does not store this data: the form only prepares the message and opens your own email app — nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email.
  • Browsing: like any website, the hosting server keeps standard technical logs (IP address, date and time, page visited and browser) for security purposes.
  • Your privacy choice: saved in your browser's local storage only so we don't ask again. It is not a cookie and is never sent to us.

3. Why we use it and legal basis

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare a proposal for your event — based on your consent and on steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).
  • Access logs: to keep the website secure and running — our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We don't use your data for advertising, we don't build profiles and we never sell data to anyone.

4. Cookies and third-party content

This website doesn't use its own cookies, analytics tools or advertising pixels. Fonts, images and most videos are served by the website itself.

  • Instagram: some videos use Instagram's official embedded player (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) and only load when you click to watch. If you choose “Essential only”, we ask for confirmation before loading it. Instagram Privacy Policy.
  • External links (Instagram and email) take you to services with their own policies, which apply once you visit them.

Done — we'll ask again on your next visit.

5. Who the data is shared with

Messages you send stay in the band's email provider or Instagram inbox. If your event goes ahead, some details may be shared with the people directly involved in organising it (for example, your wedding planner or the venue), always with your knowledge.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as needed to handle your inquiry and, if your event goes ahead, for the duration of the agreement and related legal obligations. You can ask for your messages to be deleted at any time.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask at any time to:

  • access the data we hold about you;
  • correct incomplete or outdated data;
  • delete data you sent us or restrict its use;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • object to its use or withdraw your consent.

Just write to theambassadors.liveshow@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

8. Security

The website is served over HTTPS and doesn't store form data in any database.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of the page shows the version in force.
