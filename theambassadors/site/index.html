About

About The Ambassadors

A live music experience shaped around the moment.

Party

Mood Makers

The Ambassadors Live Show brings live music into the heart of an event, creating a connection between the performers, the audience and the atmosphere of each occasion.

Through performance, musical expression and a carefully curated presentation, the band aims to make every appearance an experience worth remembering — from an intimate ceremony with piano and voice to a full show with live horns, percussion and voices that keeps the dance floor full until the very last song.

Based in Italy, the band performs at villas, castles and hotels across the country and abroad — and brings its own audio and lighting production, so every detail of the show is taken care of.

Music for your event

The right music makes every occasion more memorable.

Every event has its own atmosphere, audience and purpose. Discover how live music can become part of your celebration.

  • i. Weddings & Celebrations Create the soundtrack for meaningful moments, from elegant receptions to lively celebrations — destination weddings at villas, castles and seaside terraces.
  • ii. Private Events Bring live music to special gatherings and occasions shared with family and friends — milestone birthdays, anniversaries and parties.
  • iii. Corporate Events Add a live musical experience to corporate receptions, celebrations and selected professional events — with full audio and lighting production.
