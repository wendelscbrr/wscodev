About Emiliano Bonanomi

London-based Session Guitarist, Musical Theatre Performer, and Guildhall-Qualified Music Educator.

Over the past fifteen years, I have built a multifaceted career across the UK and Italy that bridges professional live performance, academic research, and dedicated music education. Based in Central London (Camden/Euston), I split my time between performing in musical theatre pits, playing for high-profile weddings and corporate events with function bands, and teaching guitar in schools and at my private studio.

As a performer, my work is defined by versatility, meticulous preparation, and reliability. My theatre credits include guitar chair positions for productions such as We Will Rock You, Sister Act, Rent, and The Witches of Eastwick. Whether reading complex scores in an orchestra pit using silent-stage technology or driving a packed dance floor with a funk and soul band, I always aim to serve the music and support the wider creative team.

Alongside my performing career, I am deeply committed to music pedagogy. I hold a PGCert in Performance Teaching (with Distinction) from the Guildhall School of Music & Drama and an MA in Music Performance from ICMP/UEL. As a certified Yamaha Music System tutor, IB Music Examiner, and member of the Musicians’ Union Education Committee, I have taught across respected UK music hubs including THAMES, Croydon Music & Arts (CMA), MMF, and Slough Music Service.

Beyond the stage and classroom, I actively explore the intersection of music and technology. I have collaborated with the University of Derby’s Music Technology Department on the Guitar Ambisonic (G.A.S.P.) project and co-authored the research chapter “How Blue is Our Blues? The validation of Blues in the 21st century”, published by Vernon Press.

What connects every side of my work is a belief in preparation, adaptability, and clear musical communication.

Whether I am guiding a beginner through their first chord, preparing a teenager for a Grade 8 exam, or stepping into a theatre production, my goal remains the same: to bring professionalism, energy, and genuine musical value to every project.

Languages spoken: English, Italian, and Spanish.
