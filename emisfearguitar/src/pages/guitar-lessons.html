FAQs

Questions parents & students ask

Can’t find your answer? Send me a message — I reply within 24 hours.

What ages do you teach?

I teach students from age 6 upwards — including children, teenagers, and adults of all skill levels.

Can you prepare students for official music exams?

Yes. I prepare students for Grades 1 to 8 across RSL (Rockschool), Trinity College London, ABRSM, and LCM, as well as GCSE and A-Level music performance components.

Do you offer online guitar lessons?

Absolutely. I offer both fully online and hybrid lessons, complete with high-quality audio, digital worksheets, and video feedback between sessions.

What is your cancellation policy?

At least 24 hours’ notice is required to cancel or reschedule a lesson. If I ever need to reschedule due to a live performance commitment, I always provide ample notice and rearrange the session at a time that suits you.

Where do in-person lessons take place?

At my professionally equipped home studio in the Euston / Camden area (London NW1), a short walk from Euston, Camden Town, Mornington Crescent and King’s Cross stations. The exact address is shared when you book.

Do you support students with special educational needs (SEND)?

Yes. I am SEND trained and adapt pace, materials and lesson structure to each student.

Can lessons be taught in Italian or Spanish?

Yes. Lessons are available in English, Italian and Spanish — lezioni in italiano e clases en español.
