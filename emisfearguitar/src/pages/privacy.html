Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. Who I am

This website is run by Emiliano Bonanomi (trading as EmisFearGuitar), a self-employed musician and guitar teacher based in London, United Kingdom. I am the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Contact: emisfear@gmail.com · +44 7565 016969.

2. Enquiries and lesson bookings

The enquiry form on this website does not store anything on a server. When you press Send Message or Send via WhatsApp, your device opens your own email app or WhatsApp with the message ready to send. I then receive your name, email address, phone number (if given), the subject of your enquiry and your message.

  • Why: to reply to your enquiry, arrange lessons or performances, and manage bookings and payments.
  • Lawful basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and performance of that contract (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Retention: enquiries that don't lead to a booking are deleted within 12 months; records for students and clients are kept while we work together and for up to 6 years afterwards for tax and accounting purposes.

3. Children and young students

I teach students from age 6. For anyone under 18, enquiries should be made by a parent or guardian, who provides the student's details. I only collect what is needed to teach (such as age, level and relevant learning needs, including SEND information you choose to share). Photos or videos of students are never published without the written consent of a parent or guardian.

4. Free guitar tabs mailing list

If you request the free tabs, I use your first name and email address to send you the download link and occasional emails with new tabs, videos and tips. The lawful basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to me. The list may be managed by an email marketing provider acting as my processor, under a data processing agreement. Your details are kept until you unsubscribe.

5. Cookies and third-party content

This website does not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. It stores one small preference in your browser (your choice on the privacy notice) so it doesn't ask again.

  • YouTube videos are loaded from youtube-nocookie.com only when you click play. At that point Google/YouTube may set its own cookies and receive your IP address — see Google's Privacy Policy. If you chose "Essential only", the site asks again before loading a video.
  • Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and Google Maps take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

You can change your choice at any time via .

6. Sharing your data

I never sell your data. It is only shared with service providers I use to run my business (email, messaging and, where applicable, payment providers such as your bank or PayPal), or where required by law. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK, with appropriate safeguards in place.

7. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email emisfear@gmail.com. I will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk · 0303 123 1113.

8. Security and changes

This website is served over HTTPS. The exact address of the home studio is never published and is only shared with booked students. This policy may be updated from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest version.
