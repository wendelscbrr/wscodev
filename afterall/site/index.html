AFTERAll is an electronic music project exploring the meeting point between club culture, global sounds, and the energy of the dance floor.

Blending electronic rhythms with influences from Afro House, Bolly Tech, and contemporary Indian music, AFTERAll creates a distinctive sonic experience designed to connect cultures through sound.

From original productions and creative remixes to immersive DJ sets, each project reflects a passion for musical exploration and the power of music to bring people together.

    About AFTERAll

    AFTERAll is an electronic music artist and DJ project driven by a passion for rhythm, sound exploration, and the energy of live performance.

    With a musical approach that brings together electronic club sounds and influences from Indian music, AFTERAll explores a range of styles, including Afro House, Bolly Tech, and contemporary dance music.

    The project spans original productions, remixes, collaborative releases, and live DJ sets, creating a musical identity that moves between studio experimentation and the dance floor.

    Through releases, audiovisual projects, and live performances, AFTERAll continues to develop its sound and connect with audiences through music.
