Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Who we are

This is the official website of Lillian Whitaker Music, based in Montgomery, Texas (USA). For any privacy question or request, write to whitaker.lillian12@gmail.com.

2. What data we handle

  • Contact form: name, email, subject, format, date, city/company and the message you write. The website does not store this data: the form only prepares the message and opens your own email app — nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email.
  • Browsing: like any website, the hosting server keeps standard technical logs (IP address, date and time, page visited and browser) for security purposes.
  • Your privacy choice: saved in your browser's local storage only so we don't ask again. It is not a cookie and is never sent to us.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your message and discuss the collaboration, performance or partnership you proposed — with the consent you give in the form.
  • Access logs: to keep the website secure and running.

We don't use your data for advertising, we don't build profiles and we never sell or share data with anyone.

4. Cookies and third-party content

This website doesn't use its own cookies, analytics tools or advertising pixels. Fonts and images are served by the website itself.

  • TikTok: videos use TikTok's official embedded player and only load when you click to watch. If you choose “Essential only”, we ask for confirmation before loading it. TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • External links (Instagram, TikTok and email) take you to services with their own policies, which apply once you visit them.

Done — we'll ask again on the home page.

5. Who the data is shared with

Messages you send stay in the artist's email provider or Instagram inbox. If a project goes ahead, some details may be shared with the people directly involved (for example, a producer or event organizer), always with your knowledge.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as needed to handle your message and, if a project goes ahead, for the duration of the agreement and related legal obligations. You can ask for your messages to be deleted at any time.

7. Your rights

You can ask at any time to:

  • know whether we hold your data and access it;
  • correct incomplete or outdated data;
  • delete data you sent us;
  • withdraw your consent.

Just write to whitaker.lillian12@gmail.com.

8. Security

The website is served over HTTPS and doesn't store form data in any database.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of the page shows the version in force.
