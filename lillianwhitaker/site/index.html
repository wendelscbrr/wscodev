    Meet Lillian

    57th GMA

    Dove Awards

    Music is a powerful way to express who we are, share our stories, and connect with others.

    Based in Montgomery, Texas, Lillian Whitaker is a vocalist known for lush, layered harmonies — often singing every part herself — and for heartfelt covers that move from pop and R&B classics to musical theatre, folk, country and worship.

    Welcome to the official home of Lillian Whitaker Music — a place to discover her musical journey, explore her work, and stay connected with what comes next. In 2026 she walked the red carpet of the 57th GMA Dove Awards in Nashville.
